Help Desk Agent Tools

Free practice tools for help desk agents. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Call Notes Trainer →Mock calls that build the skill of taking notes while you listen and while you talk. 4 levels, about 2 minutes per call. Use a computer with headphones. ACW Timer →Tap when the call ends. An alarm goes off before your ACW limit so you have time to wrap up. Built for your phone, with break and lunch timers.
Unofficial personal tools. Not affiliated with any employer.
