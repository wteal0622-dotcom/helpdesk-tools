Step 1 · Typing check

A 60-second typing test. If you're under 35 words a minute, or you look at the keys, start with typing practice. This skill builds on that.

Step 2 · Mock calls

Pass 2 different calls on a level to unlock the next one. A pass means you captured at least 80% of the key facts with no more than 1 repeat.

How to take notes on a call

  • Write the caller's words first. Get the problem down while they're explaining it, before you start explaining the fix.
  • Short words beat full sentences. "Pwd chg Fri from phone, locked" is a fine note.
  • Fill the template in order: Issue → Tried → Resolution → Next step. Your hands learn where things go.
  • Use the dead air. Holds, "let me look that up," and the caller logging in are free typing time.
  • When you're talking, type what they already said. Don't try to type what you're saying.
