How to use
  1. The second you hang up, tap CALL ENDED.
  2. The alarm goes off before your limit (30 seconds by default). That's your cue to wrap up your notes.
  3. Tap AVAILABLE when you go back to Available. Your time gets logged.
  4. Putting a caller on hold? Tap purple HOLD. The alarm goes off before the 3:00 max. Tap BACK WITH CALLER when you pick up. If the call ends while they're on hold, tap Call ended and your wrap-up timer starts.
  5. Keep this screen open while you work. It stays awake while a timer runs.

Add it to your home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

No sound on iPhone? Check the ring/silent switch and your volume. Tap the bell to mute the alarm. The screen will still flash.
